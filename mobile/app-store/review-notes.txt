APP STORE REVIEW NOTES
=======================
Version: 1.0.0
Build: 1
Bundle ID: xyz.degenfeed.app

LOGIN CREDENTIALS
-----------------
The app launches in read-only mode without login. To access the full social experience:
1. Tap "Connect" on the profile tab
2. Create a new Nostr keypair (no email required — keys are generated locally)
3. Or connect with an existing nsec/npub key

No email/password login is needed. The app is designed to be pseudonymous — users generate keys locally in-browser.

CRYPTO CONTENT NOTICE
---------------------
DegenFeed aggregates public posts from decentralized social protocols (Nostr, Farcaster, Lens, Bluesky). 
Content is user-generated and may include discussion of cryptocurrencies, NFTs, and blockchain projects. 
The app does not facilitate financial transactions, gambling, or trading.

AGE RATING: 17+
-----------------
We have self-rated this app as 17+ for the following reasons:
- Unrestricted web access (users can browse decentralized content)
- Frequent/intense mature/suggestive themes (user-generated content may reference crypto trading)
- Gambling: simulated/gambling references may appear in user posts (no real-money gambling in-app)

TEST ENVIRONMENT
-----------------
The production app connects to live decentralized network relays. For testing, the app can use:
- Internal test relays (ws://localhost:7777 for Nostr)
- Sandbox Farcaster hubs (configured in Settings > Developer)

KNOWN ISSUES
-------------
- First load may take 2-3 seconds as the app syncs relay connections
- Push notifications require granting permission on first launch
- Cross-posting delays: posts may take 5-10 seconds to propagate across all protocols

ADDITIONAL NOTES
-----------------
- This app does not use encryption exports (no EAR/OFAC concerns)
- All cryptographic operations use standard Web Crypto API
- No tracking or analytics SDKs are included
- The app is fully functional offline (shows cached feed)
