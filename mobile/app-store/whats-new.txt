v1.0.0 — Initial Release

Welcome to DegenFeed! This is the first release of the ultimate decentralized social feed aggregator.

• Aggregate your feed across Nostr, Farcaster, Lens, Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, and RSS
• Unified composer — write once, publish everywhere
• Real-time push notifications for mentions, replies, and likes
• Discover trending content and creators across the decentralized web
• Custom feed builder with protocol, topic, and creator filters
• Wallet connect integration for on-chain identity verification
• Dark mode optimized for late-night degen sessions
• Lightning-fast infinite scroll with virtualized lists
