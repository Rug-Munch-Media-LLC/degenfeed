[ How it works ]

01

Open the app. See everything.

No sign-up, no wallet, no key. All 7 protocols and RSS feeds load immediately. Zero friction.

02

Connect your identity.

Nostr key, Farcaster signer, Lens wallet, Bluesky password, Mastodon token — whatever you have. Sign in to like, reply, repost natively on that network. Your key never leaves your browser.

03

Cross-post everywhere.

Link multiple accounts and compose once. DegenFeed publishes the same post to Nostr, Farcaster, Lens, Bluesky, Threads, and Mastodon in parallel. Partial success? Failed networks retry in the background.

04

Ranked, transparent, yours.

Every post is scored with a public algorithm — recency, engagement, source quality, niche relevance. No black box. You can override any weight.

[ What we are ]

⊞

A client, not a network

We don't own your data. We don't host your posts. DegenFeed is a window onto the existing open social graph — we read from public APIs and sign actions with your keys.

🐘

Mastodon-compatible server

We run a GotoSocial instance at social.degenfeed.xyz. Create a free @degenfeed.xyz account in 30 seconds. Username + password, no email required. Your posts federate with the wider fediverse automatically.

◆

Open source & free

MIT license. Self-hostable. Public API. No tokens. No ads. Operating cost at scale is dinner money — we can run this forever without charging.
