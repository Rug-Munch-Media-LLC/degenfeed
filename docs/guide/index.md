# Getting Started

Welcome to DegenFeed — a multi-protocol social aggregator that brings Nostr, Farcaster, Lens, Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, Reddit, RSS, Lemmy, and more into a single unified feed.

## Quick Start

1. **Open** [app.degenfeed.xyz](https://app.degenfeed.xyz)
2. **Browse** the feed — no account required
3. **Sign in** with your Solana or EVM wallet to post, tip, and customize

## Browsing

The default feed shows a curated mix across all supported protocols. Use the filter bar to narrow by protocol, content type, or time range.

## Publishing

Connect a wallet to publish. Your posts are signed and broadcast to the protocol(s) you choose. Supported protocols for publishing: Nostr, Farcaster, Lens, Bluesky, Mastodon, and Threads.
