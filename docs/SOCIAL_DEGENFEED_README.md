# Welcome to DegenFeed Social — social.degenfeed.xyz

> **Your home base for the decentralized social web.** One account. Eight-plus networks. Your data, your rules.

---

## What is social.degenfeed.xyz?

**social.degenfeed.xyz** is the flagship entry point to the **DegenFeed Mesh** — a self-hosted constellation of decentralized social services, all linked by a single identity. It runs **GoToSocial** (a lightweight, Mastodon-compatible ActivityPub server) fronted by the **Elk** client for a familiar, polished social media experience.

When you sign up here, you don't just get a Mastodon account. You get:

- **GoToSocial** (`@you@social.degenfeed.xyz`) — Microblogging. Mastodon-compatible. Follow anyone on the fediverse.
- **Bluesky PDS** — Your own AT Protocol personal data server. Independent from Bluesky's hosted infrastructure.
- **Nostr Relay** — A dedicated relay for your notes, zaps, and nostr-native interactions.
- **PeerTube** (`@you@video.degenfeed.xyz`) — Upload videos, livestream, share short-form clips.
- **Lemmy** (`@you@community.degenfeed.xyz`) — Link aggregation, discussion forums, communities.
- **Pixelfed** (`@you@photos.degenfeed.xyz`) — Photo sharing — Instagram, but federated.
- **WriteFreely** (`@you@blog.degenfeed.xyz`) — Long-form blogging. Publish and syndicate.
- **Owncast** (`@you@live.degenfeed.xyz`) — Live streaming with OBS. Chat, tips, webhooks.
- **Mobilizon** (`@you@events.degenfeed.xyz`) — Event management. Create, RSVP, discover.
- **Castopod** (`@you@podcast.degenfeed.xyz`) — Podcast hosting. Distribute everywhere.
- **Funkwhale** (`@you@music.degenfeed.xyz`) — Music sharing and listening.
- **BookWyrm** (`@you@books.degenfeed.xyz`) — Book reviews, reading lists, discovery.
- **Matrix** (`@you@matrix.degenfeed.xyz`) — Encrypted chat and messaging. Bridged to everything.
- **Ghost CMS** (`@you@articles.degenfeed.xyz`) — Full-featured newsletter and blog publishing.
- **RSS/Atom feeds** — Every service auto-generates feeds for your content.

All with the **same username**. One registration, instant provisioning.

## How to use this page

The Elk client you're looking at is a full Mastodon-compatible interface. You can:

- **Browse the federated timeline** — see posts from users on any ActivityPub server (Mastodon, Threads, PeerTube, Pixelfed, etc.)
- **Follow anyone** on the fediverse — Mastodon users, Threads users, PeerTube creators, Pixelfed photographers, WriteFreely bloggers
- **Post updates** that federate to your followers across all networks
- **Engage** — like, reply, boost, bookmark, share
- **Manage your account** — settings, filters, lists, blocks, mute

## Moderation & community guidelines

This instance follows the [DegenFeed Community Guidelines](https://app.degenfeed.xyz/community-guidelines):

- **No spam, scams, or phishing.** Zero tolerance.
- **No harassment, hate speech, or targeted abuse.**
- **No illegal content.** We cooperate with law enforcement where required.
- **NSFW content** must be marked with a content warning.
- **Be excellent to each other.** We're building the open social web together.

Reports are handled by the admin team at Rug Munch Media LLC. To report abusive content, use the report function in the Elk interface or email **admin@degenfeed.xyz**.
